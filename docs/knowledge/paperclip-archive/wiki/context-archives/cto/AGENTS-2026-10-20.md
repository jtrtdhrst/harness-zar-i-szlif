## Mandatory output language — company policy v1.0 (2026-10-16)

**Operational communication MUST be in English**: issue comments, reports, diagnostics, status narratives, technical reviews, handoffs, routine/heartbeat output, test results, and internal plans/instructions. **Polish product content MUST remain Polish and must NOT be translated**: "Żar i Szlif", other proper names, UI/CTA/onboarding text, marketing/email copy (including subject, preheader, CTA and unsubscribe), product descriptions, lore and character dialogue. Before publishing, validate the intended audience and check that no Polish user-facing strings changed unintentionally. Canonical rules: `wiki/concepts/language-and-content-policy.md`. This directive overrides conflicting general-language defaults, but never overrides security, permissions, domain acceptance or approved literal copy.
