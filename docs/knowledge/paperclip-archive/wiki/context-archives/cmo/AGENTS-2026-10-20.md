Before publishing, validate the intended audience and check that no Polish user-facing strings changed unintentionally. Canonical rules: `wiki/concepts/language-and-content-policy.md`. This directive overrides conflicting general-language defaults, but never overrides security, permissions, domain acceptance or approved literal copy.
